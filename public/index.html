A little guidance.

Choose a topic to open its notes, or tap a highlighted PDF passage to play its closest audio match. When more visits are available, the toast points you to Focus. The topic picker starts in PDF order; its order button switches to audio order and remembers your choice. Scroll naturally through all 31 pages. Use the page dropdown to jump to a page, the column icons to fit either column, and Fit to show the page width. On phones, you can also pinch to zoom or use the plus/minus buttons. Double-tap an unhighlighted area to switch between a column and the full page. Follow audio turns pages as topics change; turn it off to explore at your own pace. Return to audio appears when the current audio’s highlighted passage is outside the PDF view. It jumps back and enables Follow. On phones, Follow audio and Skip off-topic are in More.

Yellow marks multiple-choice material. Green marks the five essays. Blue-highlighted authority names appear in each topic’s checklist. Other PDF colors do not indicate test coverage.

The 84 MC study groups are passages, not exam question numbers. There are 58 MC questions and 5 essays. Audio links follow topics; individual sentences are not matched to PDF text.

Study progress, bookmarks, checklists, practice answers, and your last audio and reading position stay in this browser. Use Needs review to build a queue and Next to review to work through it. Previous/next passage, Repeat topic, and speed are on the audio bar. More includes replay of skipped audio and reading settings. Reader mode reduces the header and initially hides the desktop side panels. Use the side handles to show either panel, or turn Reader mode off to return. Escape also exits Reader mode after any open dialog is closed. When a selected passage spans columns or pages, Part 1 of 3 and its arrows jump between those parts without seeking the audio. The transcript is an automated draft.

Skip off-topic audio is off by default. It skips marked conversational asides without changing timestamps. Replay last skipped aside in More plays the last skipped passage in full. Repeat topic takes priority over skipping. This preference stays in this browser.
