 MAKE THE DESK YOURS

A little guidance.

Choose a topic to open its notes, or tap a highlighted PDF passage to play its closest audio match. When more visits are available, the toast points you to Focus. The topic picker starts in PDF order; its order button switches to audio order and remembers your choice. Scroll naturally through all 31 pages. Use the page dropdown to jump to a page, the column icons to fit either column, and Fit to show the page width. On phones, you can also pinch to zoom or use the plus/minus buttons. Double-tap an unhighlighted area to switch between a column and the full page. Follow audio turns pages as topics change; turn it off to explore at your own pace. Return to audio appears when the current audio’s highlighted passage is outside the PDF view. It jumps back and enables Follow. On phones, Follow audio, Skip off-topic, and Review mode are in More. The Focus panel uses the same player as the main window. Each audio visit has a paired PDF location button that opens the notes without seeking audio.

The audio timeline shows the current chapter in gold; darker gold is the portion already played. Small lines separate chapters. Hover over the bar to see a chapter’s title, focus, start and end times, and duration. On a phone, touch and scrub the bar to see the same preview. The compact chapter counter shows elapsed / remaining time, with a minus sign for time remaining. Both clocks change together. The outer time labels refer to the full recording.

Yellow marks multiple-choice material. Green marks the five essays. Blue-highlighted authority names appear in each topic’s checklist. Other PDF colors do not indicate test coverage. On page 8, the Divrei Malkiel passage is included in Essay 1, with his name in blue as a required authority. A marked correction on page 16 replaces the incorrect paragraph about aging Stam Yeinam in your house: it is ASUR according to the Shulchan Aruch (SA).

The 84 MC study groups are passages, not exam question numbers. There are 58 MC questions and 5 essays. Audio links follow topics; individual sentences are not matched to PDF text.

Study progress, bookmarks, checklists, practice answers, playback speed, and your last audio and reading position stay in this browser. Use the flag under Tag for follow-up to mark Needs review. Review mode plays all linked visits for marked topics in audio order, plays shared audio once, and stops at the end. Notes-only topics remain available in the study map. Turn Review mode off to play unmarked passages. The PDF/Audio control remembers your preferred map order; opening the map or changing order reveals your selected topic. Tap the Focus title to find it in the map. Previous/next passage, Repeat topic, and speed are on the audio bar. Previous restarts the current passage; within the first three seconds, it goes to the preceding passage. With off-topic skipping enabled, those three seconds begin at the first playable audio. More includes replay of skipped audio and reading settings. The downward handle on the audio bar hides the full player and gives that space back to the notes. While audio plays, a floating mini player shows the current topic, time, pause, and expand controls. When paused, use Show player to restore the full controls. The hidden-player preference stays in this browser. Reader mode reduces the header and initially hides the desktop side panels. Use the side handles to show either panel, or turn Reader mode off to return. Escape also exits Reader mode after any open dialog is closed. When a selected passage spans columns or pages, Part 1 of 3 and its arrows jump between those parts without seeking the audio. The transcript is an automated draft.

Skip off-topic audio is off by default. It skips marked conversational asides without changing timestamps. Replay last skipped aside in More plays the last skipped passage in full. Repeat topic takes priority over skipping. This preference stays in this browser. Download audio saves the complete cleaned recording with chapter titles and PDF page references. Playback settings do not remove audio from the download. The chapter index links back to the corresponding notes and audio positions; chapters may display differently in different audio apps.
